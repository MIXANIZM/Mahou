Manual conversion stability hardening applied.

- repeated Insert conversions are serialized;
- too-fast repeats are ignored during a short bounded cooldown;
- Microsoft Word receives a longer 300 ms cooldown;
- the tracked word is copied before layout conversion;
- low-level hook, global hotkeys and raw input are restored through finally;
- cleanup failures are isolated and logged without suppressing the original error.
